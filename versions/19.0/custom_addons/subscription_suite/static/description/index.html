Subscription Suite for Odoo 19 Community

Enterprise-grade subscription lifecycle, recurring billing, dunning, portal recovery, analytics, deferred revenue, observability, and migration tools for Odoo 19 Community.

Included Capabilities

Subscription Operations

Plans, recurring orders, renewal and upsell quotes, seats, add-ons, tiered pricing, usage metering, and time-limited discounts.

Billing Recovery

Idempotent recurring billing, payment attempts, backup-token fallback, dunning policies, and manager recovery drilldowns.

Analytics

Generated MRR, retention, forecast, ARPU, LTV, churn, top-plan, at-risk, trial-conversion, and payment-recovery reports.

Finance

Deferred revenue schedules, recognition preview, journal posting, credit-note adjustments, reconciliation, and scheduled posting controls.

Operations

Unified operational runs, optional failure digest, retention cleanup, and audited CSV migration/backfill workflows.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community.
  • Correct users, companies, journals, accounts, payment providers, products, taxes, and scheduled actions before production use.
  • Accounting policy review before enabling revenue recognition in production.
Known limitations include no provider-specific webhooks, no normalized multi-currency analytics, no public usage ingestion API, no portal seat/add-on/usage/promotion self-service, and no automated migration rollback.
